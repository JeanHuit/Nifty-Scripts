Understanding Electricity ⚡

The Water Analogy

Voltage (V) = Water Pressure (the push)

Current (I) = Water Flow (how much flows)

Resistance (R) = Pipe Width (opposition to flow)

Ohm's Law (Your New Best Friend)

V = I × R

Voltage = Current × Resistance

This is like knowing how fast your EV3 motors spin!
Everything in electronics follows this rule

Why the resistor?
LED needs ~20mA, without resistor it would burn out!
R = (9V - 2V) ÷ 0.02A = 350Ω (use 470Ω)

Breadboards: Your New Building Platform 🍞

🧱 LEGO Connectors

🍞 Breadboard

Push-in connections
Temporary & flexible
Infinite configurations
Quick prototyping

Breadboard Rules:

• Rows connect horizontally (1-5, 6-10)

• Power rails connect vertically

• Center gap separates left/right

• No connection between different rows

💻 Arduino Programming

void setup() {
  pinMode(13, OUTPUT);
}

void loop() {
  digitalWrite(13, HIGH);
  delay(1000);
  digitalWrite(13, LOW);
  delay(1000);
}

Same logic, different language!
